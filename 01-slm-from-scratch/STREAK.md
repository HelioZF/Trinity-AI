## ✅ What counts as a day
- ⚡ A quiz / gap re-test round with Claude
- 📖 One Codex section read + a one-line takeaway told to Claude
- ✍️ A handwritten mini-summary from memory
- 🐛 A bug-hunt round, or any small build/code step
- 🏗️ Weekend: the week recap + deep dive

## 📜 Rules
1. One micro-task = the day counts. More is welcome, never required.
2. Missed a day with no freeze left → streak resets to 0. (That's what makes it work.)
3. The freeze renews every Monday. Claude applies it automatically to the first missed day of the week.
4. Milestones: **7** 🏅 · **14** 🥈 · **30** 🥇 · **60** 🏆 · **100** 👑 — celebrated properly.
